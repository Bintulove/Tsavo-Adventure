# Introduction

AI fluency is not just about accuracy—it is about relevance, accessibility, and trust.

In African classrooms, students learn better when examples reflect local realities, familiar language, and community experiences. Generic AI outputs often fail because they rely on Western assumptions that do not fit East African environments.

Using the Tsavo 4D Framework, EduSavvy can build culturally grounded, curriculum-aligned, and inclusive AI learning systems.
